Features

What is actually in Terrabloque 1.0.5.

Huge worlds

Standard worlds are 65,536 x 65,536 blocks, 16x bigger than Beta. Beta is 16,384 and Alpha is 2,048. Flat worlds too. Far Lands wait at the edge.

Creative & Survival

Pick a game mode, a seed (or roll the dice), difficulty, cheats, TNT explosions, keep inventory and structures when you create a world.

Nine biomes

Ocean, forest, birch forest, taiga, plains, desert, jungle, mountains and savanna.

Mobs

Chickens, pigs, cows, sheep and villagers share the land with zombies, spiders, mummies and slimes of three sizes.

Fishing & boats

Craft a Fishing Rod (3 Sticks + 2 String), cast, wait for the bite and reel in fish, junk or rare treasure. Build a Boat and sail.

Build with shape

Stairs and slabs in wood, cobblestone, sandstone and brick. Torches and Sea Lamps glow in your hand, and TNT, fire and water all behave.

Deserts & the deep

Desert pyramids and Egyptian temples, and the Thalassoterra underwater city. Use /map to find your way.

Commands

Chat with T and use /tp, /give, /kill and /map when cheats are on.

Your game, your way

Quality presets, languages (English, Español Argentina, Thai), and resource packs in the launcher version.

How to Play

Terrabloque runs on desktop and on phones.

Desktop

WASD: walk
Space: jump
Ctrl: sprint
Shift: crouch
Left-click: break
Right-click: place
Wheel or 1-9: switch block
E: block menu
T: chat, / for commands
Esc: settings

Phone

Drag: look around
Tap: place a block
Hold: break a block
Arrow pad: move
Hold a hotbar slot: drop
Double-tap up and hold: sprint

Tips

Double-tap jump to fly in Creative. Caves hide ore, bring torches.
